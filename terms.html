Basic and optional Pro subscriptions

Existing Basic decision, Talk, Vault, rating and saved-history capabilities remain available without a Pro subscription. Where offered, Pro adds alternative-priority exploration, shareable decision reports and category-specific analytics. The App Store displays the actual localized price and billing period before you confirm a purchase. Monthly and annual subscriptions, when available, renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation. Manage or cancel your subscription in Apple Account settings; restore eligible purchases using Restore purchases in the app. Apple processes billing and refunds under its applicable terms. An unresolved or unverified purchase does not grant Pro access. Ending a subscription removes access to the additive Pro tools, not your original saved Basic records.
