Privacy policy

Your choices stay yours.

Effective November 19, 2026 · Decision Layer iOS

Decision Layer helps you organize personal choices and evidence. The current native app is designed around local, owner-scoped records, and its core workflows do not require a developer-managed account.

Data you choose to store

When you use the app, your device may hold your questions and Talk drafts, options, personal ratings, choices, action and outcome notes, source links, selected photos and files, place associations and app preferences. These records may be personal or sensitive. The current native core keeps these records on your device rather than uploading them to a Decision Layer-operated server. Device-only Keychain information is used to separate local owner records; it is not a cross-device backup.

Device permissions

  • Camera, Photos and Files: used when you actively choose a photo, video or document to save or examine. The app should not access your entire library just to show the file picker.
  • Microphone and speech: used after you initiate a voice action and grant the required permission. On-device speech handling depends on the available Apple frameworks and device configuration.
  • Location: requested when you choose a nearby place or map action. The app does not require continuous background location permission for its core decision workflow.

Apple services and external actions

When you choose Apple Maps, location lookup, speech recognition or an external URL/share action, the operating system, Apple or the service you open may process the query, location or content under its own privacy terms. We do not promise these services operate entirely offline or that external websites retain nothing.

Analytics, ads and tracking

In the current native source, no developer-operated analytics upload, third-party advertising SDK or cross-app tracking SDK is included in the core app and Share Extension. We do not sell your saved decisions or original files. Apple and the App Store may separately process purchase, device or diagnostic data under their own policies.

Saving, deleting and recovering

Saved local data stays in the app's on-device storage until the app or user changes or deletes it. Some information can be removed using the relevant in-app controls. Deleting an app or resetting an iPhone may have different effects on app files, device-only Keychain entries and operating-system backups; recovery of records from a prior device or older test build is not guaranteed. If you see unreadable older data, preserve the original installation instead of reinstalling or inventing a new owner identity.

Earlier TestFlight versions

Pre-release versions may have different storage formats or optional integrations. This policy describes the current audited native app; it does not certify that historical external accounts, earlier hosted features or cloud copies never existed or have been removed. For help with historical test data, contact Support without posting any private data.

Changes to this policy

If features change materially, including the addition of developer-managed accounts, cloud sync, subscriptions or third-party SDKs, this page and the app's App Store privacy disclosures need to be reviewed and updated before release.

Questions and contact

Visit Decision Layer Support for questions or to request a private channel. Public GitHub Issues are not a safe place to share personal records.

Transparency: Device-local storage is not the same as a verified backup. Always retain your original iPhone and its app if you are relying on older private Vault or Keychain data.
