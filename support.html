Common questions

Where are my decisions saved?

The current native app uses on-device storage for its core decision, Talk and Vault records. The appearance of an empty view does not prove an older on-device record was erased or backed up.

Should I reinstall to fix missing history?

No. If an important earlier installation shows missing or unreadable history, preserve that iPhone and its installed app. Original Keychain and in-place upgrade recovery need verification before reinstallation, migration, data reset or cleanup.

Why doesn't a location or voice feature work?

Location and microphone permissions are optional and requested for specific actions. Apple Maps and Apple speech services also depend on device, region and service availability. Inspect iOS app permissions; do not share private audio or addresses in a public request.

How do I make or correct a choice?

Open Decisions, add your own options and priorities, then record an action or outcome. Saved originals and corrections require your explicit review. The app doesn't invent paid transactions, visits or ratings.

How do subscriptions and purchase recovery work?

If Pro plans are available, choose Settings, then Subscription. The App Store supplies the localized price and billing period. Selecting a plan is not a purchase; confirmation happens through Apple. If a purchase is awaiting approval, keep using Basic and do not start another purchase until the earlier request is resolved. A local unresolved-request reminder may survive a restart. Restore purchases checks verified access. If you have confirmed the earlier request is resolved but the reminder remains, use the recovery action in the subscription screen to check access again. Manage or cancel in Apple Account settings. For billing or refund questions use Apple billing support. Do not post receipt or account details publicly.

What happens when Pro ends?

After the App Store no longer verifies access, the additional Pro tools become unavailable. Original Basic decisions, ratings, Talk, Vault and saved history remain accessible. Do not reinstall or delete your local records to fix a subscription problem.

Can I share a completed decision?

Where the report feature is available, open Decision Follow-through and preview the shareable summary. Private planning and outcome notes are excluded by default; opt into them only if you want to share them. Check the exact preview before choosing a destination. No original attachment is included automatically.
